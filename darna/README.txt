DARNA Mehres Storefront

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract the ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Internet is needed for Google Fonts and the illustrative Unsplash images.

BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace illustrative images with actual photos of your mehres products.
- Add confirmed prices, sizes, materials, delivery fees, return policy, privacy policy and contact details.
- Open index.html and find WHATSAPP_NUMBER near the end. Add the business WhatsApp number with Tunisia country code 216, digits only.

This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript storefront prototype. The form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message; it does not store orders or process payments. Real checkout requires backend/order storage and a payment provider.
